01 / OUR MISSION

Talent is everywhere.
Research access
should be too.

Undergraduates who participate in research gain experience, skills, and clearer paths into the careers they imagine. Yet the first opportunity can be hard to reach—especially without an existing network. ScaleSTEM is here to help bridge that gap through equitable mentorship and a project students can call their own.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Not just “research experience.” A body of work that tells your story.

By the end of a cohort, students aim to have a defined question, a technical project in progress, a research report, and practice presenting their work. The exact project and pace are shaped with a mentor around each student’s interests and starting point.
