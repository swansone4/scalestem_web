Refinement criteria in incompressible CFD

Cells are flagged for refinement where the vorticity gradient exceeds a tolerance, concentrating resolution in the wake.

Research Advisor

How do I know where my mesh needs refining?

Flag cells where the vorticity gradient is large, like the wake behind your airfoil. Refine those, then compare lift before and after.
