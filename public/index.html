003/ THE PATHWAY

From where you are
to where you want to go.

Three steps connect your starting point, a guided research project, and the opportunities you want to pursue.

01 / QUESTIONNAIRE

Map your direction.

Share your current skills, experience, interests, and target roles. The questionnaire helps identify where you are and what you need to build next.

02 / THREE MONTHS

Learn, then build.

Follow a structured learning and research pipeline. Build the methods and computational project that match your direction, with mentor guidance along the way.

  1. MONTH 01ScopeSet a question and learn the foundations.
  2. MONTH 02ModelBuild, test, and refine the project.
  3. MONTH 03ReviewAnalyze findings and write the report.

03 / OPPORTUNITY PIPELINE

Put the work to use.

Identify aligned internships, research projects, and modeling or simulation roles. Use your project and reviewed report to make a focused application for each.
