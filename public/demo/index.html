Core theory

A uniform mesh wastes cells where the flow is calm and starves the wake where it isn’t. Adaptive refinement splits only the cells where the solution changes fastest. For incompressible flow we solve

(1)∇ · u = 0
(2)∂u∂t + (u · ∇)u = −1ρ∇p + ν∇2u

On each cell K of size hK we estimate the local error with a vorticity indicator, then flag the worst cells:

(3)ηK = hK ‖∇ω‖K,   refine K if ηK > θ · maxJ ηJ

Halving h on a scheme of order p cuts the error by about 2p, which is why refining the right fraction of cells can beat refining all of them:

(4)eh ≈ C hp  ⇒  eheh/2 ≈ 2p

Why the vorticity gradient and not just velocity?

Velocity is smooth across most of your wake. Vorticity changes sharply at the shear layers, so its gradient points straight at the cells your lift estimate depends on.
