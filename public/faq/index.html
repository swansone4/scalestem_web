The short version of what ScaleSTEM is, what you walk away with, and what it costs.

    ScaleSTEM is a guidance platform for computational research projects. We help the next generation of STEM talent build the skills, and the proof of those skills, that high-signal internships, jobs and fellowships ask for.

    Most project platforms hand everyone the same template, with little sense of who you are or where you want to go. We start by asking what matters most for your career, then structure everything around it: the project, the lessons, and the roles you aim at.

    Typical platformSame project for everyoneGeneric course listYou figure out the career part
    ScaleSTEMProject built from your goalLessons your project needsOutreach and roles aimed at your goal

    You build a computational research project in a STEM field that fits your goal. Over about 3.5 months, roughly the length of a summer internship or fellowship, it turns into:

    • { }A fully documented, evidenced GitHub repository
    • PDFA technical report on your methods and results
    • JA publication in the ScaleSTEM Computational Research Journal

    Along the way you use your new skills to reach out to R&D scientists, professors and academics, with the aim of getting into a real research group. We also guide you through hundreds of targeted, high-signal jobs, internships and fellowships matched to your goals.
